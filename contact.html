FAQ

Common questions

What is the human economy?

The human economy is a new way of thinking about personal development. Instead of investing in companies, you invest in people—their goals, their growth, their potential.

How does my stock value work?

Your stock value starts at an IPO price and fluctuates based on your goal completion, streak maintenance, and investor confidence. Complete goals consistently to increase your value.

Is Pipoll free to use?

Yes! Pipoll is free to download and use. You can track habits, build your profile, and invest in others without any cost.

How many habits can I track?

You can track up to 4 custom habits at a time. Choose habits that matter most to your growth and focus on consistency.

What happens if I break a streak?

Your stock value may decrease, but you can always rebuild. The key is to never miss twice—get back on track immediately.

Can I see other users' progress?

Yes! The social market lets you browse other users' stocks, see their goals, and invest in people you believe in.
