Verification help

Why didn't my photo verify?

Most failed checks are simple photo issues — not you doing anything wrong. Check these first.

The #1 cause

📷 Photo quality

Most failed checks come down to one of these four. Fix the photo, not the habit.

❌
Too dark

Turn on a light or move near a window. Dim photos are automatically rejected.

❌
Blurry

Hold still a second before tapping. Motion blur hides the details we need.

❌
Too close

A tight crop of fabric, skin, or a page could be anything. Step back and show context.

❌
Wrong angle

Shoot the way the instruction says. It's written that way for a reason.

Framing

🖼️ Not enough proof in frame

The verifier needs to see the habit, not guess it.

If your photo shows the room but not the activity — or the object but not the place — it fails. Show both.

Zero exceptions

📱 Things that always fail

Rejected by design — every time, no matter how honest the attempt.

Screens

TV, laptop, tablet, another phone. Even showing something real.

Prints

Photos of posters, prints, magazines, or book covers.

Apps

Bible apps, e-readers, piano apps, online classes. Physical only.

Per habit

✋ Quick fixes

What a passing photo looks like, habit by habit.

🏋️ Gym

Equipment + space + mirrors = pass.

🧴 Skincare

Product on your skin or bottle open in frame. Hovering hand + closed bottle fails.

🌿 Grass

Fingers actually in the grass, blades bending. Hovering fails. Artificial turf fails.

📖 Reading

Book must be open, close enough to read the page layout. Covers and Kindles fail.

📚 Class

Shoot from your seat — desk in front, board/screen ahead. Hallway or empty room fails.

🎸 Instrument

Hand on the strings/keys, instrument in playing position. On the wall or in its case fails.

🛏️ Bed

Whole bed in frame from the foot. Don't worry about wrinkles — real beds have them.

⚽ Sport

Gear + court/field/poolside + you on it. Watching from the stands fails.

🍳 Cooking

Food mid-cooking, pan and stove visible. Plated delivery food fails.

📝 Study

Open notebook or book with real writing, pen out. Laptop alone fails.

One more try

🔄 Still failing?

Retake once with more light and more context in frame.

If a genuinely honest photo keeps failing, that's on us, not you — report it and we'll review it. Your streak matters to us too.
