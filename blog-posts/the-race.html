Philosophy 13.8M Years Ago By Markus

The Race

A Brutal Taxonomy of Ambition and how to outperform society

Picture this: Seven billion people at the starting line. Patterns suggest four categories of participants.

The Cockroaches (20%)

Invisible until lights turn on. "If I can't win, I'll make sure you lose too." They monitor others but produce nothing. Scattered across every industry, every social circle, every family.

The Sheep (70%)

The comfortable majority. "I'm just living my life." They avoid change and remain adequately unhappy rather than seeking improvement. They follow, they consume, they comply.

The Honey Bees (9%)

Disciplined workers who lose value. "Hard work pays off... right?" They build for systems rather than building their own systems. Essential, replaceable, exhausted.

The Wolves (1%)

The apex performers. "I hunt, therefore I am." They prioritize possibility over reason and build empires while others make excuses. They don't compete — they dominate.

How to Become the Wolf

  1. Develop Predatory Clarity — Know exactly what you want. Eliminate ambiguity.
  2. Build Compounding Systems — Work that works while you sleep.
  3. Eliminate Comfort-Seeking Habits — Comfort is the enemy of growth.
  4. Embrace Strategic Isolation — Wolves hunt alone. Sheep travel in herds.
  5. Develop Selective Aggression — Pick your battles. Win them decisively.
  6. Track Everything — What gets measured gets improved.
  7. Never Defend, Only Execute — Results silence critics better than words.

Conclusion

Inequality is inherent. You're in the race whether you like it or not. Choose your animal quickly.

Disclaimer: IQ obviously doesn't play a role at all in any of this. All correlations are coincidental.
